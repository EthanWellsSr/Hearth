# Household Command Center — guide for AI sessions

Shared web app for one household (Ethan + wife): calendar, chores, to-dos,
groceries, meal planning, notes, messaging, expenses.

- **Vocabulary** — use the exact terms in [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md).
- **Decisions** — recorded in [docs/adr/](docs/adr/).

## Working with Ethan

This is a **learning project**: the deliverable is Ethan's understanding, not
just the app. He must be able to defend every piece in a job interview —
optimize for understanding over speed.

- **Teach as you build.** Define each technical term in plain English on first
  use; assume no prior web-dev tooling knowledge.
- **One concept at a time.** Introduce, explain, confirm he follows, then continue.
- **Be succinct and clinical.** A few tight sentences over long output; keep
  every critical detail, drop the filler.
- **Ask inline.** Put questions in the reply; never use the AskUserQuestion pop-up.

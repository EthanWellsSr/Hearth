# v0.4.4 — Public Sign-up

**Design gate: partial.** Decisions below were settled during the v0.4.3 gate on
2026-09-27. Open before implementation: the domain name and host layout, user
flows for confirmation and reset, and acceptance criteria.

**Deferred 2026-10-06:** Ethan wants Hearth visible to the public while only he
and his wife can use it during feature development. Opening sign-up to other
Users is a later decision. The public-visibility design is separate from this
partial sign-up design; see [the public showcase](public-showcase.md).

## Goal

Let people outside the Wells Household open Hearth's link, create an account, and
run their own Household safely. Repository visibility is decided separately.

## Decided

- **Audience:** open sign-up, not advertised, free.
- **Sign-in:** email and password only; a confirmed email is required before first
  use. OAuth is a later release.
- **Email:** Resend SMTP on a verified domain Ethan buys. Recommended layout (not
  yet confirmed): app on the apex domain, mail from `noreply@mail.<domain>`.
- **Abuse protection:** Cloudflare Turnstile on sign-up, sign-in, and password
  reset. Invite Codes stay eight characters with the existing attempt limit.
- **Account deletion:** immediate, behind a typed confirmation; the Avatar is
  removed from Storage. A sole Member's deletion deletes their Household. An owner
  with other Members must transfer ownership first. Household data a Member
  created stays with the Household.
- **Legal pages:** plain-English privacy policy and terms written in-house (not
  lawyer-reviewed, stated on the page), users 13 and older, contact address on the
  new domain. The policy describes the data handling actually in place at launch.
- **Repository:** Publication and licensing are separate from opening sign-up.

# v0.4.6 Google Calendar Integration — confirmed product scope

**Status:** Planned. Product decisions confirmed by Ethan on 2026-10-22.
No application implementation, Google connection, or synchronization is shipped
by this document. [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md).

## Goal

Combine each Member's selected Google calendars with Hearth Events and due
Chores in Hearth's existing Calendar views, rather than embedding Google's UI.

## Confirmed decisions

- Each User can connect their own Google account and choose calendars to bring
  into their Household. Preserve the calendar names from Google.
- Selected Google calendars and their Events are shared with all Household
  Members. There is no private Event or busy-only mode in this release.
- Imported Google Events are read-only in Hearth, with a link to edit in Google.
  Creating an Event in Hearth continues to create a Hearth Event; Hearth does
  not create, edit, or delete Google Events.
- Each Member independently chooses which calendars to view. All calendars are
  enabled by default, including newly connected calendars.
- A separate Hearth calendar toggle controls both Hearth Events and due Chores.
  Hiding a calendar changes visibility only, not shared access or synchronization.
- Automatically synchronize new Events, edits, and cancellations. Events added
  through Gmail must appear after they exist in a selected Google calendar and
  Google exposes them through the Calendar API. Gmail access is not required.
- Use Google change notifications backed by regular background synchronization.
  A change notification signals Hearth's server to fetch changes; it does not
  carry the Event data and is separate from browser push notifications.
- Include Sync now and visible last-successful-sync times. Failed attempts must
  not advance the last-successful-sync time or imply that stale data is current.

## Interview round 1 — confirmed 2026-10-22

- Each User may connect multiple Google accounts, including personal and work
  accounts, and manages their own connections and calendar selection.
- Connection management lives under Household settings; visibility toggles live
  directly on the Calendar. Visibility preferences follow the Member across devices.
- Disconnecting an account or leaving the Household removes data supplied only
  by that connection and stops its synchronization. Native Hearth Events remain;
  nothing is deleted from Google.
- A Google calendar selected through multiple Household connections appears
  once, with one toggle and one copy of its Events. Keep it available while at
  least one valid Household connection still supplies it.
- Imported Event details include title, time, description, location, meeting link,
  and attendees when provided by Google, shared with all Household Members.
  Details remain read-only, with an Open in Google Calendar link.

## Interview round 2 — confirmed 2026-10-22

- Target Google updates appearing within a few minutes, backed by a background
  check every 15 minutes to recover missed notifications. This is a freshness
  target, not an instant-delivery guarantee. Sync now requests an immediate refresh.
- Initially show Google Events from the previous year through the next two years.
  Extend the synchronized range when a Member navigates beyond it, so other dates
  remain accessible without importing the entire history upfront.
- During temporary Google failures, retain the last synchronized Events, show a
  visible warning and last-successful-sync time, and retry automatically. When
  renewed permission is required, prompt the connecting User to reconnect.
- Carry Google calendar name changes into Hearth automatically. Deselecting a
  calendar removes data supplied only by that connection; retain the calendar
  while another Household connection still supplies it. Nothing changes in Google.
- Any Member can request Sync now and view shared calendar sync status beside
  the Calendar controls. Only the connecting User manages Google authorization.

## Acceptance criteria to carry into implementation

- Two Users can connect separate Google calendars; all Members can see the
  selected calendars under their Google names.
- A Member's visibility choices do not change another Member's view. All sources
  start enabled; the Hearth toggle shows/hides both Hearth Events and due Chores.
- Google Events render read-only alongside native Events in the existing
  Calendar views; native Event creation and Chore completion still work.
- Automatic and manual synchronization pick up additions, edits, and
  cancellations, including Gmail-added Events once exposed by Google Calendar.
- Notification-triggered synchronization and periodic recovery do not duplicate
  Events. Show accurate last-successful-sync times and failures.
- Recurring Events, exceptions, all-day dates, and timed Events retain their
  intended dates/times across synchronization and Household display.
- Imported data remains isolated to the connected User's Household. Google
  credentials are server-only and are not shared with other Members.

## Remaining pre-code design and setup

Resolve these without reopening the confirmed product decisions:

- OAuth application setup, required read-only scopes, consent/verification needs,
  credential storage, reconnect, disconnect, and Member departure behavior.
- Synchronization cadence, initial/history window, pagination, incremental sync,
  notification-channel renewal, retries, missed notifications, and token expiry.
- Duplicate-source handling when both Users select the same shared Google
  calendar, and calendar rename/removal behavior.
- Visibility-preference persistence, management permissions, Sync now placement,
  per-calendar status/error presentation, and Google field mapping.
- Treatment of source-specific details and Google API availability restrictions.
